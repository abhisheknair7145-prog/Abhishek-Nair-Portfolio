02 / Research

From Waste to Wealth

MSc dissertation · Birmingham City University

Dissertation project

How Reverse Logistics Drives Competitive Advantage in E-Commerce Supply Chains

This dissertation examined reverse logistics in e-commerce and explored its operational challenges and potential benefits for businesses.

Reverse logisticsE-commerceCircular economySustainability

Research approach

The study used a pragmatic, deductive approach and mixed methods, combining a Microsoft Forms survey with asynchronous written interviews.

Survey responses: 27. The survey included multiple-choice questions and short-answer questions.

Challenges identified

  • Product quality uncertainty: 13 respondents (48.1%)
  • High operational costs: 12 respondents (44.4%)
  • Technology and information gaps: 8 respondents (29.6%)

Potential benefits reported

  • Resource efficiency: 10 respondents (37%)
  • Waste reduction: 8 respondents (29.6%)

Percentages describe responses in this study; they should not be interpreted as representative of all e-commerce businesses.

Skills applied

Research planning, survey design, written-interview collection, organising responses, Excel analysis, PivotTables, and communicating findings.
